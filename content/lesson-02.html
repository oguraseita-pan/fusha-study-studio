Core model: '+ar('أُرِيدُ + قَهْوَةً','ar big-ar')+' = I would like coffee.

Spoken vs. written Fuṣḥā
For correct written MSA, the direct object is often accusative: قَهْوَةً. Do not stop a beginner’s conversation just to correct its final -an; speak first, then review endings.

MON · ROLE-PLAY

Make your order your own

Each round: teacher first, then swap roles. Keep dialogues short and clear.

ROUND 1 · SUPPORTED

Order one drink

Customer chooses tea / juice / water / coffee. Server asks for one detail; customer answers politely. Success: one clear order + one answer

ROUND 2 · HIDE THE MODEL

Change two details

Teacher quietly changes the drink and temperature. Student orders again without reading. Success: two full sentences without hints

ROUND 3 · SURPRISE

New choice

Server has no hot tea. Customer chooses cold coffee instead. Use simple language and ask one question. Success: one choice + one question

TEACHER GUIDE

A clear speaking-first method

Use the existing textbook for 20–30 minutes at most if it directly supports the week’s topic.

DO

  • Speak in clear, simple Fuṣḥā*.
  • Ask who / what / how much / do you have*.
  • Wait for the student to form an answer.
  • Recycle Week 01 words (work, live, study, I/you).
  • Repeat role-plays with a changed detail.
  • Correct 2–3 important errors, then repeat the dialogue.

AVOID

  • Long English grammar lectures.
  • Reading dialogues for the entire 2-hour class.
  • Teaching many new foods without practice.
  • Correcting every final case vowel during speaking.
  • Changing to Syrian dialect without labeling it.
  • Letting the teacher speak almost all the time.

Easy English instructions for the teacher

  • “First, order one drink.”
  • “Now change to cold coffee.”
  • “Ask me how much it costs.”
  • “You are the server now.”
  • “I do not have that drink. Choose another.”
  • “Try again without looking.”

LEARNER NOTES · 日本語

初心者がつまずきやすい文法ポイント

授業本編は英語＋アラビア語です。難しい点だけ自習用に日本語で補足します。

① 「欲しい」＋名詞は、目的語を取る

أُرِيدُ قَهْوَةً は「私はコーヒーが欲しい」です。厳密なフスハーでは目的語が対格なので、語尾のタンウィーンは -an になります。ただし会話練習中はまず語順・意味を優先し、格語尾の修正はあとにしましょう。

② 形容詞は名詞の性に合わせる

قَهْوَة（コーヒー）は女性名詞、شَاي（お茶）は男性名詞です。例えば「冷たいコーヒー」なら قَهْوَة بَارِدَة、「冷たいお茶」なら شَاي بَارِد。名詞・形容詞をセットで練習すると定着しやすいです。

③ 「丁寧さ」は長い文章でなくても出せる

مِنْ فَضْلِكَ（男性相手）／مِنْ فَضْلِكِ（女性相手）を加えるだけでも丁寧になります。フスハーの教材として自然に使えます。ただし現地では湾岸方言や英語もよく使われるので、先生には口語とフスハーの違いも短く教えてもらいましょう。

④ 目的は台本の暗唱ではない

毎回「飲み物」「温度」「砂糖」「値段」「売り切れ」の条件を少し変えます。覚えた単語を組み合わせ、先生の質問に即答できることを優先してください。
