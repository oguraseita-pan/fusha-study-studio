Yesterday & Past Events

昨日のこと・過去形 · 7 · 9 · 11 Dec · Three 120-minute classes

أَمْسِ وَالْفِعْلُ الْمَاضِي

Say what you did yesterday using simple past verbs.

Learning goals & review

Say what you did yesterday using simple past verbs.

Recall first: Ask a question from the previous week's role-play. Describe yesterday in 2 sentences. Change one person's name, time or place.
Grammar focus: Perfect past: ذَهَبَ he went; ذَهَبْتُ I went; ذَهَبْتَ you (m.) went; ذَهَبَتْ she went. Negative past: مَا ذَهَبْتُ = I did not go.

Fuṣḥā is the target. Use simple English for teacher instructions. Tap Arabic script for device speech synthesis (when available).

Six high-use phrases

PHRASE 1
مَاذَا فَعَلْتَ أَمْسِ؟
What did you do yesterday? (to m.)
昨日何をしましたか？
PHRASE 2
ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الْعَمَلِ.
I went to work.
仕事に行きました。
PHRASE 3
دَرَسْتُ الْعَرَبِيَّةَ.
I studied Arabic.
アラビア語を勉強しました。
PHRASE 4
مَا ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى السُّوقِ.
I did not go to the market.
市場には行きませんでした。
PHRASE 5
أَكَلْتُ مَعَ صَدِيقِي.
I ate with my friend.
友人と食事しました。
PHRASE 6
ثُمَّ رَجَعْتُ إِلَى الْبَيْتِ.
Then I went home.
それから帰宅しました。

Model dialogue 1

A
مَاذَا فَعَلْتَ أَمْسِ؟
What did you do yesterday?
B
ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الْعَمَلِ.
I went to work.
A
هَلْ دَرَسْتَ الْعَرَبِيَّةَ؟
Did you study Arabic?
B
نَعَمْ، دَرَسْتُ فِي الْمَسَاءِ.
Yes, I studied in the evening.

Role-play missions

Mission 1: Tell your teacher three things you did yesterday.
Mission 2: Describe a meal using two past verbs.

Teacher: speak clearly, keep English simple, and do not correct every minor mistake during the role-play. Give three key corrections at the end, then let the student retry.
