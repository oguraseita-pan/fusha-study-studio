CORE GRAMMAR

The present tense: who does the action?

Use the beginning of the verb (prefix), and sometimes its ending, to identify the person.

Example verb: to work · past (he): عَمِلَ · present (he): يَعْمَلُ
I work
أَنَا أَعْمَلُ
anā aʿmalu
أَ / a-
We work
نَحْنُ نَعْمَلُ
naḥnu naʿmalu
نَ / na-
You work (man)
أَنْتَ تَعْمَلُ
anta taʿmalu
تَ / ta-
You work (woman)
أَنْتِ تَعْمَلِينَ
anti taʿmalīna
تَ...ينَ / ta-...īna
He works
هُوَ يَعْمَلُ
huwa yaʿmalu
يَ / ya-
She works
هِيَ تَعْمَلُ
hiya taʿmalu
تَ / ta-
Key rule
أ / ن / ت / ي are the main prefixes. The vowel inside the verb is not always the same. Learn the present form together with its past form. Example: دَرَسَ ← يَدْرُسُ.
Important contrast
The form تَعْمَلُ can mean you work (man) or she works. The pronoun and the situation tell you which. For you (woman), use تَعْمَلِينَ.
CORE GRAMMAR

Two easy sentence patterns

Do not try to add case endings perfectly when you speak. First make a complete, clear sentence.

PATTERN 1 · A fact with no “am / is / are”
أَنَا يَابَانِيّ.
I am Japanese.
anā yābāniyy.
هِيَ مُدَرِّسَة.
She is a teacher.
hiya mudarrisa.

In the present tense, many Arabic noun sentences have no word corresponding to “am / is / are”.

PATTERN 2 · Pronoun + present-tense verb + place / object
أَنَا أَعْمَلُ فِي شَرِكَةٍ يَابَانِيَّةٍ.
I work at a Japanese company.
anā aʿmalu fī sharika yābāniyya.
أَنَا أَدْرُسُ الْعَرَبِيَّة.
I study Arabic.
anā adrusu al-ʿarabiyya.
Two useful tools: هَلْ تَعْمَلُ؟ = Do you work? (man) · لَا أَفْهَمُ. = I do not understand.
Put هَلْ before a yes/no question; put لَا before a present verb to negate it.

WORD BANK · 7 NOUNS

Nouns: gender + plural

Arabic nouns have grammatical gender. Plurals can be regular or irregular.

اِسْم↔أَسْمَاءsingular / plural
namemasculine
مَا اسْمُكَ؟What is your name? (man)
شَرِكَة↔شَرِكَاتsingular / plural
companyfeminine
هَذِهِ شَرِكَةٌ يَابَانِيَّةٌ.This is a Japanese company.
مَكْتَب↔مَكَاتِبsingular / plural
officemasculine
أَنَا فِي الْمَكْتَب.I am in the office.
بَيْت↔بُيُوتsingular / plural
house / homemasculine

WORD BANK · 7 NOUNS

Nouns: gender + plural

Focus on the singular now; begin to recognize the plural.

عَمَل↔أَعْمَالsingular / plural
work / jobmasculine

Plural tip
Some plurals change the inside of the word (e.g. بَيْت ← بُيُوت). Do not invent a plural; memorize it with the noun.

MON · ROLE CARDS

Three rounds of introductions

Each round lasts 6–8 minutes. The teacher should change details and add one new question.

ROUND 1 · Supported

New classmates

Student: You have joined an Arabic course. Tell the teacher your name, country, city and what you study. Ask the same questions.

Teacher: Ask short questions and allow reference to the word bank.

ROUND 2 · No notes

First day at an office

Student: Introduce yourself to a new colleague in Dubai. Explain your job in one simple sentence. Ask where the colleague lives and works.

Teacher: Give a different name, country and job. Ask “What do you study?”

ROUND 3 · Surprise

Meet a visitor

Teacher: You have just moved from another country. Ask two unexpected but simple questions.

Student: Try to continue without English. If you are confused, use “Again, please”.

After class: 60-second speaking record

Say five facts about yourself without reading. Leave space for three phrases to repair tomorrow.

Talk about your weekday

WED · ROLE-PLAY + LISTENING

From routine to real conversation

Make the student ask questions, not only answer them.

ROUND 1 · Supported

Compare two workdays

Teacher: say you work in the morning. Student: describe your own day. Ask each other 4 questions: where, when, what, do you ...?

ROUND 2 · No notes

Change the schedule

Teacher: say you start work in the evening and study Arabic in the morning. Student: ask for details and compare the two routines.

ROUND 3 · Information gap

Find three differences

Teacher secretly chooses three changes (city, time, activity). Student asks Arabic questions to discover them.

Teacher mini-listening script (read twice)

TEACHER
اسْمِي سَامِي. أَسْكُنُ فِي دُبَيّ. أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَكْتَب صَبَاحًا. أَعْمَلُ فِي شَرِكَة. أَعُودُ إِلَى الْبَيْت مَسَاءً.
My name is Sami. I live in Dubai. I go to the office in the morning. I work in a company. I return home in the evening.

Check: Where does Sami live? When does he go to work? What does he do in the evening?

FRI · SURVIVAL LANGUAGE

Keep the conversation going

These are fixed phrases. Learn them as chunks; detailed grammar can wait.

لَا أَفْهَمُ.
I do not understand.
lā afhamu.
مَرَّةً أُخْرَى، مِنْ فَضْلِكَ.
Again, please. (to a man)
marratan ukhrā, min faḍlika.
بِبُطْءٍ، مِنْ فَضْلِكَ.
Slowly, please. (to a man)
bibuṭʾin, min faḍlika.
مَاذَا يَعْنِي هَذَا؟
What does this mean?
mādhā yaʿnī hādhā?
كَيْفَ أَقُولُ هَذَا بِالْعَرَبِيَّة؟
How do I say this in Arabic?
kayfa aqūlu hādhā bil-ʿarabiyya?
هَلْ يُمْكِنُكَ أَنْ تُعِيدَ السُّؤَال؟

hal yumkinuka an tuʿīda as-suʾāl?
Teacher gender
For a female teacher, change the polite ending from فَضْلِكَ to فَضْلِكِ. The full question “Can you repeat?” also changes: هَلْ يُمْكِنُكِ أَنْ تُعِيدِي السُّؤَال؟.

FRI · ROLEPLAY CHALLENGES

Three no-notes speaking tasks

Each challenge takes 8–10 minutes. Teacher adjusts only one difficulty at a time.

TASK 1 · Meet a colleague

Introduce + ask back

TASK 2 · Coffee break

Routine + simple preferences

Teacher: talk about a workday and ask where / when / what. Student: respond and invite the teacher for coffee using هَلْ تُرِيدُ قَهْوَةً؟. Success: 4 connected answers.

TASK 3 · Unexpected question

Repair + continue

Teacher: speak a little faster and ask one new question. Student: ask for a repetition or a slower explanation instead of switching immediately to English. Success: 2 repair phrases used naturally.

TEACHER GUIDE

Please teach in clear Modern Standard Arabic

Short sentences, easy explanations, many student speaking turns.

DO

  • Let the student answer before helping.
  • Recycle the same 24 core words all week.
  • Model male and female question forms.
  • Change names, times and places often.
  • Correct 2–3 important errors, then repeat the task.

AVOID

  • Long grammar lectures in English.
  • Changing to Syrian dialect without labeling it.
  • Asking only yes/no questions.
  • Correcting every small case ending.
  • Finishing all tasks without checking speaking.

Minimal teacher language (simple English)

  • “Listen first. Now repeat.”
  • “Good. Now say it with I.”
  • “Ask me the same question.”
  • “One more time, without looking.”
  • “Can you say it another way?”
  • “Try again. The beginning of the verb is different.”

Two gender-specific models

أَيْنَ تَسْكُنُ؟
Where do you live? (to a man)
أَيْنَ تَسْكُنِينَ؟
Where do you live? (to a woman)
مَا اسْمُكَ؟ / مَا اسْمُكِ؟
What is your name? (to a man / to a woman)
Pronunciation priority
The student can use simple MSA without pronouncing all final case vowels. In a pause, endings are often not pronounced. Focus first on the verb prefix and understandable meaning.
TEACHER GUIDE

Answer key + common corrections

Use the answers as models; similar correct sentences are acceptable.

1 · I live in Dubai
أَسْكُنُ فِي دُبَيّ.
2 · I study Arabic after work
أَدْرُسُ الْعَرَبِيَّة بَعْدَ الْعَمَل.
3 · He works in a Japanese company
هُوَ يَعْمَلُ فِي شَرِكَة يَابَانِيَّة.
4 · Where do you study? (man)
أَيْنَ تَدْرُسُ؟
5 · She studies Arabic every day
هِيَ تَدْرُسُ الْعَرَبِيَّة كُلَّ يَوْمٍ.
6 · I do not understand. Again, please.
لَا أَفْهَمُ. مَرَّةً أُخْرَى، مِنْ فَضْلِكَ.

Common mistakes to watch

  • I / he: أَعْمَلُ vs. يَعْمَلُ.
  • You man / woman: تَدْرُسُ vs. تَدْرُسِينَ.
  • In / to: فِي دُبَيّ vs. إِلَى الْمَكْتَب.
  • Questions: أَيْنَ = where, مَتَى = when.
LEARNER NOTES · 日本語

文法上の注意（自習用）

先生と共有する授業本編はアラビア語＋やさしい英語です。

① 未完了形の接頭辞

現在形（未完了形）は、基本的に接頭辞で人称を区別します。私＝أَ、私たち＝نَ、あなた／彼女＝تَ、彼＝يَ。ただし実際の母音は動詞により変わり、女性への「あなた」には ـِينَ の語尾も必要です。

② 完了形と未完了形をセットで暗記

例えば「勉強する」は دَرَسَ（彼は勉強した）／يَدْرُسُ（彼は勉強する）／أَدْرُسُ（私は勉強する）です。未完了形では語幹の母音が一律ではないため、辞書形だけで覚えないことが大切です。

③ 「話せること」を優先

文法書では格語尾やタンウィーンも学びますが、今回の授業ではまず「質問を理解する」「人称を正しく変える」「文章をつないで話す」ことに重点を置きます。フスハーであっても、語末で止めるときに格語尾が省略されることがあります。

④ その週の優先事項

月曜に覚えた表現を水曜・金曜でも必ず使い、先生の質問に対して日本語や英語に翻訳せず答える練習をします。最終目標は、金曜に資料なしで3分程度の簡単なやり取りを続けることです。
