FUSHA SPEAKING LAB · WEEK 5

Time, Plans & Appointments

時間・予定・約束 · 30 Nov · 2 Dec · 4 Dec · Three 120-minute classes

الْوَقْتُ وَالْمَوَاعِيدُ

Ask about time, agree on a meeting, and change a plan.

Learning goals & review

Ask about time, agree on a meeting, and change a plan.

Recall first: Ask a question from the previous week's role-play. Describe yesterday in 2 sentences. Change one person's name, time or place.
Grammar focus: Future: سَـ + imperfect verb (سَأَذْهَبُ = I will go). Ask مَتَى؟ (when?) and كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟ (what time?).

Fuṣḥā is the target. Use simple English for teacher instructions. Tap Arabic script for device speech synthesis (when available).

Six high-use phrases

PHRASE 1
كَمِ السَّاعَةُ الآنَ؟
What time is it now?
今何時ですか？
PHRASE 2
هَلْ أَنْتَ مُتَاحٌ غَدًا؟
Are you free tomorrow? (to m.)
明日は空いていますか？
PHRASE 3
عِنْدِي مَوْعِدٌ.
I have an appointment.
約束があります。
PHRASE 4
هَلْ تُنَاسِبُكَ السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ؟
Does 3 PM work for you?
3時は都合がいいですか？
PHRASE 5
سَأَتَّصِلُ بِكَ غَدًا.
I will call you tomorrow.
明日電話します。
PHRASE 6
نَلْتَقِي فِي الْمَسَاءِ.
We meet in the evening.
夕方会いましょう。

Model dialogue 1

A
مَتَى أَنْتَ مُتَاحٌ؟
When are you free?
B
أَنَا مُتَاحٌ يَوْمَ الْإِثْنَيْنِ.
I am free on Monday.
A
هَلْ تُنَاسِبُكَ السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ؟
Does 3 PM work?
B
نَعَمْ، هَذَا يُنَاسِبُنِي.
Yes, that works.

Role-play missions

Mission 1: Ask a colleague to meet on Monday.
Mission 2: Change the time twice. Teacher refuses one option.

Teacher: speak clearly, keep English simple, and do not correct every minor mistake during the role-play. Give three key corrections at the end, then let the student retry.
