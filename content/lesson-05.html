Grammar lab · Apply the rules

1. Time question
كَمِ السَّاعَةُ الآنَ؟

What time is it now? Use السَّاعَةُ + ordinal: السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ means 3 o'clock.

2. Future prefix
أَذْهَبُ ← سَأَذْهَبُ

The prefix سَـ comes before the entire imperfect verb. It does not replace the person prefix أَـ.

3. Appointment noun
عِنْدِي مَوْعِدٌ.

عِنْدِي (I have) + noun. Change to عِنْدَكَ for 'you have' (to a man).

4. Gender in questions
أَنْتَ مُتَاحٌ / أَنْتِ مُتَاحَةٌ

To a man: anta mutāḥun; to a woman: anti mutāḥatun. Adjectives agree with the person.

Quick teacher drills

Drill 1: Give the student three available times and make one impossible.
Drill 2: Ask for an earlier appointment and then a later one.
Drill 3: Do a 60-second voice-only phone call arranging a meeting.

Independent production & lesson review

Task 1 (no notes): Student creates four personal statements and four questions related to this week's topic.

Task 2 (surprise): Teacher changes one piece of information each turn (person, location, time, or reason).

Task 3 (recall): Retrieve five expressions from earlier lessons and weave two into the current role-play.

Feedback: The teacher writes three corrections in simple English. The student records the same 60-second task again, applying corrections.

Homework: 5 min aloud after class, 5 min next day, 5 min after 3 days. This is a practice routine, not a guarantee of retention.
