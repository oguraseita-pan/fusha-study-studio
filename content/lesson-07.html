FUSHA SPEAKING LAB · WEEK 7

Hotel, Home & Requests

ホテル・住まい・依頼 · 14 · 16 · 18 Dec · Three 120-minute classes

الْفُنْدُقُ وَالْبَيْتُ

Check in, describe problems, and politely ask for help.

Learning goals & review

Check in, describe problems, and politely ask for help.

Recall first: Ask a question from the previous week's role-play. Describe yesterday in 2 sentences. Change one person's name, time or place.
Grammar focus: Possession: عِنْدِي حَجْزٌ (I have a booking). A problem: الْمُكَيِّفُ لَا يَعْمَلُ (The AC does not work). Question: هَلْ يُمْكِنُ ...؟ (Is it possible ...?).

Fuṣḥā is the target. Use simple English for teacher instructions. Tap Arabic script for device speech synthesis (when available).

Six high-use phrases

PHRASE 1
عِنْدِي حَجْزٌ.
I have a reservation.
予約しています。
PHRASE 2
أَيْنَ الْمِصْعَدُ؟
Where is the elevator?
エレベーターはどこですか？
PHRASE 3
الْمُكَيِّفُ لَا يَعْمَلُ.
The AC does not work.
エアコンが動きません。
PHRASE 4
أَحْتَاجُ إِلَى مُسَاعَدَةٍ.
I need help.
手助けが必要です。
PHRASE 5
هَلْ يُمْكِنُ تَغْيِيرُ الْغُرْفَةِ؟
Can the room be changed?
部屋を変更できますか？
PHRASE 6
أَيْنَ الِاسْتِقْبَالُ؟
Where is reception?
フロントはどこですか？

Model dialogue 1

A
مَسَاءُ الْخَيْرِ. عِنْدِي حَجْزٌ.
Good evening. I have a booking.
B
أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا. مَا اسْمُكَ؟
Welcome. What is your name?
A
اسْمِي كِنْجِي.
My name is Kenji.
B
غُرْفَتُكَ فِي الطَّابِقِ الثَّالِثِ.
Your room is on the third floor.

Role-play missions

Mission 1: Check in using a reservation name.
Mission 2: Ask about your room and the elevator.

Teacher: speak clearly, keep English simple, and do not correct every minor mistake during the role-play. Give three key corrections at the end, then let the student retry.
