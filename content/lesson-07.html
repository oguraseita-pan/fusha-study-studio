Hotel, Home & Requests

Check in, describe problems, and politely ask for help.

Learning goals & review

Check in, describe problems, and politely ask for help.

Recall first: Ask a question from the previous week's role-play. Describe yesterday in 2 sentences. Change one person's name, time or place.
Grammar focus: Possession: عِنْدِي حَجْزٌ (I have a booking). A problem: الْمُكَيِّفُ لَا يَعْمَلُ (The AC does not work). Question: هَلْ يُمْكِنُ ...؟ (Is it possible ...?).

Fuṣḥā is the target. Use simple English for teacher instructions. Tap Arabic script for device speech synthesis (when available).

Grammar lab · Apply the rules

1. Noun gender
غُرْفَةٌ جَدِيدَةٌ

غُرْفَةٌ is feminine; adjectives typically add ـة to agree.

2. Plural
غُرْفَةٌ → غُرَفٌ

Some Arabic plurals change internally. Memorize the plural together with the singular.

3. Problem statement
الْمُكَيِّفُ لَا يَعْمَلُ.

Negate an imperfect verb with لَا. The verb itself keeps its normal imperfect form.

4. Polite request
هَلْ يُمْكِنُ تَغْيِيرُ الْغُرْفَةِ؟

Use هل يمكن + verbal noun for a polite request. The question is more formal than a direct command.

Quick teacher drills

Drill 1: Pretend the door is broken; ask the student to report it.
Drill 2: Offer a repair tomorrow when the student wants it today.
Drill 3: Ask the student to switch from a hotel to an apartment scenario.

Independent production & lesson review

Task 1 (no notes): Student creates four personal statements and four questions related to this week's topic.

Task 2 (surprise): Teacher changes one piece of information each turn (person, location, time, or reason).

Task 3 (recall): Retrieve five expressions from earlier lessons and weave two into the current role-play.

Feedback: The teacher writes three corrections in simple English. The student records the same 60-second task again, applying corrections.

Homework: 5 min aloud after class, 5 min next day, 5 min after 3 days. This is a practice routine, not a guarantee of retention.
